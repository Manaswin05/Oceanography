About OceanVision

YOLOv8 Detection

Real-time object detection localises the fish within the image, producing bounding boxes and initial class predictions at ~60 FPS on GPU.

MobileNetV2 Classifier

A fine-tuned MobileNetV2 backbone classifies fish at the species level. Trained on Indian Ocean species data from CMLRE research vessels.

OpenCV Feature Analysis

30 morphological, colour and texture features are extracted: body shape, fin geometry, eye position, colour distribution, and scale texture.

Oceanographic Mapping

Habitat polygons, migration route polylines, and species density heatmaps are rendered on Leaflet.js with Esri Ocean Base tiles.

CMLRE Research Data

Species distribution data integrated from CMLRE (Centre for Marine Living Resources & Ecology) otolith collection — Indian Ocean deep-sea surveys via R/V Sagar Sampada.

Conservation Awareness

Every species card displays IUCN Red List status. The map highlights threatened species to support marine conservation decision-making.
